Executive Summary

Marshmallow, Mars, Incorporated – a global leader in confectionery, petcare, and nutrition – faces a rapidly evolving market with rising consumer demands for personalization, sustainability, and operational excellence. Leveraging SAP’s comprehensive AI ecosystem (Joule, AI Core, Build Apps, Generative AI Hub) offers transformative opportunities across Finance, Materials Management, Sales & Distribution, Human Resources, and the SAP Business Technology Platform. This catalogue identifies 20 prioritized AI use cases, each mapped to business processes, technology layers, ROI estimates, complexity ratings, and prerequisites, enabling Mars to accelerate digital transformation while maintaining responsible AI governance.

SAP AI Landscape Overview

  • SAP Joule: Data‑centric compute platform enabling real‑time AI analytics and automated data pipelines.
  • SAP AI Core: End‑to‑end AI platform for model training, governance, and deployment across SAP S/4HANA and BTP.
  • SAP Build Apps AI: Low‑code application development with AI‑powered UI, NLP, and workflow automation.
  • Generative AI Hub: Plug‑and‑play generative models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) integrated via APIs for rapid prototyping.

These pillars interlock to provide Mars with a unified, secure, and scalable AI ecosystem, ready for both predictive analytics and generative experiences.

Responsible AI – Draft Considerations

  • Transparency: Model documentation, explainability dashboards for key decision points.
  • Fairness: Bias audits for supplier risk and credit scoring models.
  • Privacy: GDPR and CCPA compliance for customer data in GenAI applications.
  • Accountability: AI steering committee with cross‑functional representation.
  • Security: Zero‑trust API gateways for GenAI integration.
  • Governance: AI Core model registry with versioning and rollback.

All AI initiatives will be reviewed by the Responsible AI Office before production rollout.
